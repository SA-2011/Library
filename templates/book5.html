Ten blacks

**And Then There Were None** (formerly published under the title **Ten Little Niggers** in some countries) was written by Agatha Christie and was first published in **1939**.

It is **one novel** (not a series). It is one of the best-selling mystery books ever written and tells the story of ten strangers invited to an isolated island where they begin to die one by one.
